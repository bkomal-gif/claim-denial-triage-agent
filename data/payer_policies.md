# Payer policy excerpts (SYNTHETIC, for demonstration only)

## CO-16 - Claim lacks information
Claims denied for missing or invalid information should be corrected and resubmitted as a corrected claim. Do not file a formal appeal; a corrected claim is processed faster.

## CO-4 - Procedure code inconsistent with modifier, or required modifier missing
Bilateral or side-specific procedures (e.g. joint injections) require an anatomical modifier such as RT or LT. Add the correct modifier and resubmit as a corrected claim.

## CO-18 - Exact duplicate claim/service
The service has already been adjudicated. No further action is needed; verify payment on the original claim and close the denial.

## CO-29 - Time limit for filing has expired
Claims must be filed within the payer's timely filing limit. An appeal is accepted only with proof of timely submission, such as a clearinghouse acceptance report. Without proof, the balance is written off and cannot be billed to the patient.

## CO-50 - Non-covered: not deemed a medical necessity
Appeal with supporting clinical documentation: physician notes, prior conservative treatment tried, imaging or test results, and how the service meets the payer's coverage criteria. Without documentation, an appeal is unlikely to succeed.

## CO-197 - Precertification/authorization/notification absent
If an authorization was obtained but not attached, appeal with the authorization number. If no authorization exists, some payers allow retro-authorization within a short window; otherwise the balance is written off.

## CO-97 - Payment included in the allowance for another service (bundling)
If the service was separate and distinct (different session, site or lesion) and this is documented, appeal or resubmit with modifier 59 or an X{EPSU} modifier. If it was not distinct, the bundling is correct and the line is written off.
